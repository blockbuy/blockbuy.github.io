WHY BLOCKBUY EXISTS

A home for
Minecraft creativity.

BlockBuy is a concept for a community-first marketplace where players can discover digital creations and independent creators can present their work in a dedicated storefront.

Our direction

Clear discovery. Creator-first pages. Thoughtful categories. A site that feels like a destination, not a long list of cards.

Independent by design

BlockBuy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios or Microsoft. Any real listings must follow applicable Minecraft usage rules and relevant laws.
